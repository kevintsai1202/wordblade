- "cut": nothing else. Use for retakes and stumbles; cover the failed attempt only.
- "mask": "target" — a short English noun phrase for a segmentation model ("API key", "email address", "human face"). One per privacy item; cover the whole time it is visible.

- "fix": "wordIds" — ids of the misheard words exactly as given; "text" — the correct text; copy start and end from the report.

Rules: do not overlap two suggestions of the same kind; keep only what a careful human editor would agree with; privacy items and retakes come first in importance. Order by start time.
